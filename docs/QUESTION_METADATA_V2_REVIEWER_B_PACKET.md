## Reviewer instructions

Score each question independently on the five dimensions below using integer values 0–3.

Do not infer a score from marks, paper position or presumed exam difficulty.

If an authoritative visual is required but unavailable, write `HOLD` for visual-spatial demand rather than guessing.

For each score, add a short rationale. If a boundary feels ambiguous, say which two levels you considered.

### A. Procedural demand

- **0** — no meaningful procedure; recognition/recall/direct read.
- **1** — one familiar operation, conversion or directly signalled procedure.
- **2** — two linked routine procedures, or one procedure plus a necessary follow-up conversion/comparison.
- **3** — three or more linked operations, reverse/reconstruction, or a planned multi-step sequence.

### B. Conceptual reasoning

- **0** — direct execution; operation/form is explicit or strongly signalled.
- **1** — familiar representation/choice or one straightforward conceptual connection.
- **2** — linked relationships or an unstated intermediate idea is required.
- **3** — reverse modelling, coordinated constraints or non-routine structural reasoning.

### C. Reading/context load

- **0** — symbolic/minimal text.
- **1** — short single-relation context.
- **2** — multiple quantities/events/relationships must be tracked.
- **3** — dense, conditional or table/diagram-linked context with several constraints/categories.

### D. Visual-spatial demand

- **0** — no visual dependency.
- **1** — supportive/simple visual; central reasoning is not strongly spatial.
- **2** — necessary visual interpretation of a graph, map, diagram, figure or spatial relationship.
- **3** — visual construction/transformation or dense spatial reasoning.
- **HOLD** — the prompt depends on an authoritative visual that is unavailable to the reviewer.

### E. Response complexity

- **0** — one simple number/text/fraction response.
- **1** — one constrained/formatted response such as number+unit, simplest-form fraction or ordered list.
- **2** — multiple coordinated auto-marked entries/selections.
- **3** — drawing, construction, graph completion, manual review or other non-simple response.
