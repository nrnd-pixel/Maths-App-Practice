# V3.2B.2 canonical curriculum topics

V3.2B.2 uses a single topic vocabulary for student filtering, question editing and imports. Known older labels are mapped automatically.

| Strand | Legacy / alternate label | Canonical label |
|---|---|---|
| Number | Rates, Ratio | Rates & Ratio |
| Number | Powers and Roots | Powers & Roots |
| Number | Fractions, Decimals and Percentages | Fractions, Decimals & Percentages |
| Measurement | Capacity, Volume | Capacity & Volume |
| Statistics & Data | Average | Averages |
| Statistics & Data | Tally Charts | Tables & Tallies |
| Mathematical Thinking & Problem Solving | Number Patterns | Patterns & Rules |
| Mathematical Thinking & Problem Solving | One-step Word Problems | One-step Problems |
| Mathematical Thinking & Problem Solving | Multi-step Word Problems | Multi-step Problems |

`Algebra`, `Powers & Roots`, `Fractions, Decimals & Percentages`, and `Pictorial Algebra` are included directly in the canonical student topic menus because they occur in the PSR 2025 bank.
